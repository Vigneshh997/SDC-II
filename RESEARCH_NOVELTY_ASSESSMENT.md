# Research novelty assessment

Search date: 31 October 2026. This is a targeted primary-source review, not a systematic review or guarantee of priority. Searches covered multimodal chest radiograph classification, image/report contrastive learning, EHR fusion, metadata-conditioned learning and report leakage; sources include author papers, proceedings, provider documentation and official repositories. Publication status below distinguishes proceedings/journal work from preprints. No competing paper's scores are imported into X-Fusion results.

DAViT remains the visual methodological foundation, not the only relevant comparator. Its single-image pediatric binary task is distinct from the proposed adult multi-label clinical-context task. BiomedCLIP + CXR-BERT + MLP + cross-attention + InfoNCE + ASL + LayerCAM/SHAP is presently a coherent engineering integration, with **no established algorithmic novelty or demonstrated predictive contribution**.

## Defensible hypotheses, not claims

1. **Incremental clinical utility:** correctly timed structured physiology contributes beyond image+indication, rather than merely reflecting referral bias. Test ITM vs IT, I, T, M and TM on the identical cohort, with subgroup support and paired intervals. Age/sex-only metadata is a much weaker premise than measured physiology; report exactly which fields were observed.
2. **Fusion benefit:** current cross-attention improves performance/calibration at a comparable training budget over concatenation and late fusion. Hold encoders and preprocessing fixed, report parameter/time differences, and retain negative findings. Distinct encoder names do not explain a gain.
3. **Contrastive mechanism:** tri-modal alignment benefits scarce-label prediction without forcing clinically similar patients apart. Compare all-pair InfoNCE, no-NCE and image/text-only alignment; stratify by metadata similarity and examine gradient/input sensitivity. One study per patient prevents repeated-patient false negatives but not identical age/sex or shared disease semantics across patients.
4. **Explanation usefulness:** class-specific spatial sensitivity and conditional patient attribution identify meaningful evidence beyond attention weights. Require independent localization annotation, perturbation/randomization controls and explicit noncausal scope. Causal or “faithful clinical reasoning” claims remain unsupported.
5. **Practical efficiency:** a frozen-encoder method retains useful performance with 8 GB VRAM. Report measured time/memory/accuracy trade-offs and cache storage, including cold-start and explanation cost. A successful artificial batch does not establish a full research training budget.

## Minimum literature-facing experiments

Prioritize a matched BiomedCLIP image baseline, strong indication-only baseline, image+text pooled/late fusion, full tri-modal model and no-contrastive/no-attention variants. Add task-adapted CaMCheX-style fusion when feasible; disclose when a full competitor cannot be reproduced due to data/compute. Include the no-CNN comparison to isolate the DAViT-inspired local refinement. Keep training information identical where comparisons are claimed to isolate architecture.

For journal-level robustness, add an independently mapped external cohort, strict timing eligibility versus explicitly untimed proxy sensitivity, masked-label analysis, calibration and patient-level confidence intervals. Do not manufacture a full-report leakage control in the clinical application; if studied retrospectively, isolate it as an invalid deployment-input upper bound and do not save it as a serving model.

## Current decision

Do not pitch X-Fusion as the first tri-modal CXR system, a novel InfoNCE method, an exact DAViT reproduction, or a validated explanation method. A potentially publishable contribution would be a rigorously measured, narrowly specified result about fusion/alignment, evidence validity or practical efficiency. Existing source code and fixtures do not yet demonstrate that result. After valid pilot data exist, retain the strongest supported question; if cross-attention/InfoNCE fails to improve the matched baseline, simplify the model and report the negative result honestly.
